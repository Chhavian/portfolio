## On Building Things

There's a specific kind of clarity that comes from being the only person responsible for something. No handoff to engineering. No design review. No one to blame for the tradeoffs but yourself.

That's what these projects are — full ownership, start to finish. The PM instinct (what problem, for whom, why now) running alongside the engineering instinct (what's the simplest version that actually works) alongside the aesthetic instinct (does this feel right).

They're also how I stay sharp. Building keeps the opinions honest.
