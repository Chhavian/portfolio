About

I'm Chhavi — a product manager with a background that sits at an intersection most people don't expect. I think in systems. I care about aesthetics. I believe these two things are not in tension.

I've shipped products in consumer, B2B SaaS, and platform contexts. What ties them together: I tend to work on the hard parts — the activation problem no one's solved, the analytics layer that keeps getting deprioritised, the feed that everyone knows is broken but nobody has fixed. I find it interesting to be the person who names the real problem before solving it.

Before product, I spent time in design research, which means I think about users with a level of granularity that most PMs don't. I've sat in enough user sessions to know the difference between what people say and what they actually mean.

I'm based in Bangalore. I read more than I talk about. I care about craft — in products, in writing, in the things I surround myself with.
